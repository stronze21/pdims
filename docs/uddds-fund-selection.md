# UDDDS: choosing funds when stock is short

Select the service date, ward and eligible items, then choose **Charge & Issue Selected**. If the current fund cannot cover the selection, choose an alternate fund in **Fund sources for selected items**. Review its available quantity and add another fund if needed before **Continue to Charge & Issue**.

An eligible standing order may show an earlier charge slip. That slip does not prevent choosing funds for the next daily dose: processing creates a separate daily order. A daily order that already has a charge still requires its charge to be adjusted separately before using another fund.

Alternate funds must have unexpired stock for the exact medicine at the current pharmacy location. Stock is rechecked during processing; current-fund stock is used first, then the selected alternate funds at their respective prices.
When no alternate fund is available, search by medicine name (at least two characters) and choose a **Replacement medicine / stock item**. The list shows the fund code, batch balance, unit price and expiry. The replacement applies to all selected patients in that medicine/fund group and retains the requested quantity. Confirm the replacement medicine, strength, form and quantity before continuing. Choose **Keep original item** to clear it. Stock is rechecked at processing, with unexpired batches allocated in expiry order within the selected medicine and fund. The standing enrollment remains unchanged; the daily dispensed order records the original medicine and fund in its remarks. Already-charged daily orders require a separate charge adjustment.

After **Continue to Charge & Issue**, changed items open **Confirm item replacement**, listing each original medicine, its replacement, fund, total quantity, affected patient count and unit price. Confirm the replacements, then confirm **Charge & issue**. Cancel either confirmation to stop before processing. Changing the selected items, replacement, funds or service date requires confirmation again.

The replacement selector and quantity explanation appear only when matching replacement stock is available (or a replacement is already selected). Before searching, they stay hidden. A search of at least two characters with no matching replacement shows **No record found**.

Replacement stock search runs when you leave the search input, rather than on each keystroke. Enter at least two characters, then click outside the input or press Tab to view results.

## Print issued orders by ward

Choose the service date and ward, then select **Print issued by ward**. Leave the ward as **All wards** to print each ward on a separate page. The print preview includes only issued UDDDS orders for that date and pharmacy location, grouped by patient, with medicine names and issued quantities. Patient names appear on the first medicine row. Pending and charged-but-unissued orders are excluded. Use **Print issued UDDDS orders** in the preview to open the browser print dialog. Queue status and item checkbox selections do not limit this report.
