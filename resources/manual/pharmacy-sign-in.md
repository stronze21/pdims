# Signing in to Pharmacy Management

1. Enter your registered email address and password.
2. Use the eye button to show or hide the password. If Caps Lock is on, a reminder appears while typing.
3. Select **Remember me** only on a device you trust.
4. Select **Sign in**. The button displays **Signing in…** while your request is submitted.

Use **Forgot password?** to start password recovery when it is available. If sign-in fails, read the error above the form, correct your credentials, and try again.

Use the sun or moon button at the top of the page to change between light and dark appearance. All PDIMS pages share this choice, including pharmacy, portal, dispensing, and queue pages. The choice is remembered in this browser and synchronized across its tabs when browser storage is available. Printed documents stay light.

For login assistance, contact the IT Department or Pharmacy Administration. This system is for authorized pharmacy personnel only; activities are logged and monitored.
